Profile

I'm Riley (they/them), a french software developer interested in distributed systems, interoperability, and backend architecture.

I enjoy understanding how systems behave once they stop being simple: when data moves between services, when failure cases matter, and when legacy components still need to coexist with newer ones.

During my spare time, I keep track of news in the tech industries. I document what I learn, and share it through public technical writing. I aim to release most of my work into the public domain.

I also enjoy exchanging with other developers by discussing their projects, asking questions about their design decisions, and receiving feedback on my own work, whether it is related to my projects or my technical writing.

Outside of programming, I enjoy literature ( currently reading Blade runner by Philip. K Dick ), mathematics and tabletop RPGs.
